                            OMRBench -- PER-GENERATOR OPERATING CHARACTERISTICS                             
============================================================================================================
12 sheets per (generator x injector) cell. Every detector sees IDENTICAL sheets.
Results are NEVER pooled across generators: a single averaged number hides
exactly the conditions under which a method fails.

  FPR    false-alarm rate on error-free sheets       (lower is better)
  POW    power, over all injected error types        (higher is better)
  AWARD  mean marks WRONGLY AWARDED per sheet        (harms integrity)
  AW@BR  the same, reweighted to a 1.8% base rate of errors
  REC    share of lost marks RETURNED vs the no-op    (higher is better)
  HOLD   mean marks WRONGLY WITHHELD per sheet       (harms the candidate)
  LOC    median localisation error, questions        (lower is better)
  CMP    decision-schema completeness, 0-1           (transparency)
  BRIER  comparative confidence metric               (lower is better)
  ECE    expected calibration error, comparative     (lower is better)

  All sheets below are SYNTHETIC. Nothing here is a confirmed historical
  case, so none of it establishes operational performance.

  BRIER and ECE rank detectors on identical data. They do NOT establish
  deployment calibration: these cells hold three error sheets per
  error-free sheet, while the reported confidence uses a 1.8% base rate
  as its prior. A base-rate-thresholded posterior cannot be validated on
  a test set whose base rate is 75%.

  Each FPR below is over 12 error-free sheets. An observed 0.000
  bounds the true rate below 22.1% at 95% confidence
  (Clopper-Pearson, exact). Zero observed is not zero risk.
